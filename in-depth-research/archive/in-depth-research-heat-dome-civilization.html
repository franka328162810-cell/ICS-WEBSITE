Under the Heat Dome: An ICS Diagnosis of the 2026 Eurasian Thermal Crisis

Between June and mid-July 2026, Western Europe experienced its most severe heatwave on record, with estimated excess deaths exceeding 10,000. The simultaneous US heat dome placed over 140 million Americans under heat alerts. World Weather Attribution confirmed: this event would have been virtually impossible fifty years ago. This paper applies the ICS Interstellar Civilization Studies framework — the New Three Views (NC, NL, NK), three core principles (RFP, NRP, REV), and six quantitative indicators — to deliver a systematic civilizational diagnosis: from the participatory epistemic error embedded in industrial civilization's cosmological self-understanding, to the multi-level negentropy crisis afflicting living systems, to the normative epistemic failure in climate governance. CSIA and CDI assessments situate the climate trajectory within ICS quantitative risk architecture, while deep-time projections from T2 to T3 illuminate the interstellar stakes. The conclusion is direct: climate change has triggered Forbidden Red Lines FRL-2 and FRL-4, placing climate governance urgency above virtually every other civilizational risk agenda.
