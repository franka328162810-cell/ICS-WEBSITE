Who Speaks for Earth?
The IAA SETI 2026 Declaration and the Question of Civilizational Authority

📅 June 13, 2026  |  ⏱️ Reading Time: 10 min  |  🏷️ SETI/METI · First Contact Governance · ICS Framework

In June 2026, the International Academy of Astronautics SETI Committee published its landmark 2026 Declaration — the most comprehensive overhaul of first-contact protocols since the 1989 post-detection protocol. Its centerpiece is a principle that sounds deceptively simple: no single nation, institution, or individual may respond to a signal on behalf of humanity. Any such decision belongs to all of Earth, mediated through bodies of the broadest possible international representation. From the vantage point of Interstellar Civilization Studies, this is not merely a procedural reform. It is the first institutional acknowledgment that contact-response is a civilizational-level act — and that humanity is not yet equipped to perform it.
