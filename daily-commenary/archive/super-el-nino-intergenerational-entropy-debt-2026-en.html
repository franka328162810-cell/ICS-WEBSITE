Super El Niño and the North American Heat Wave:
When the Intergenerational Entropy Debt Comes Due

📅 July 6, 2026 | ⏱️ Reading Time: ~15 min | 🏷️ Climate Change · Intergenerational Justice · NRP

On July 4, 2026, the 250th anniversary of American independence concluded under the hottest Independence Day on record. Washington D.C. surpassed 102°F, shattering a 120-year-old record; Philadelphia reached 103°F; at least 25 people died directly from this heat wave. Simultaneously, the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) confirmed that a super El Niño — expected to exceed all historical records in intensity — was rapidly forming in the Pacific and would intensify further through July to September. The World Weather Attribution network published its rapid attribution analysis on the same day, concluding that the strength of this heat wave was "virtually impossible" in a world without fossil-fuel-driven climate change. This is not a climate statistics report. It is a default notice on an intergenerational debt contract.
