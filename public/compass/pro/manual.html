2. Complete Metrics Reference / 完整指标参考

UCS — Universal Consciousness Spectrum / 通用意识谱系

Measures the degree of agent consciousness across 5 sub-dimensions:

DimName名称Weight
ΦPhenomenal experience现象体验0.25
TTemporal integration时间整合0.20
RReflexive modeling反思建模0.20
VVolitional autonomy意志自主0.20
SSocial-empathic社会共情0.15
UCS = (Φ^0.25 × T^0.20 × R^0.20 × V^0.20 × S^0.15) — Weighted Geometric Mean

Agency threshold: UCS ≥ 0.3 qualifies an entity as a moral patient.

RFD — Recursive Freedom Degree / 递归自由度

RFD = (|P(t₁|a)| − |P(t₀)|) / |P(t₀)|

Measures the relative change in a society's genuine choice space. Positive = freedom expanding; negative = freedom compressing. Range: (−∞, +∞).

测量社会真实选择空间的相对变化。正值=自由扩展；负值=自由压缩。范围：(−∞, +∞)。

6. FAQ / 常见问题

Q: How are sub-dimension scores combined? / 子维度分数如何合成？

UCS and CDI use weighted geometric mean (product of weighted powers). CRV and CSIA use weighted arithmetic mean (weighted sum). MBCL uses a max function across the assessment matrix. RFD is a ratio calculation. All formulas are visible in simplified form on the Results page.

Q: Is my subscription data shared? / 订阅数据会被共享吗？

No. ICS Compass runs entirely in-browser. Subscription management is separate from evaluation data. Your evaluations never leave your device.
