ICS COMPASS FREE

User Manual / 免费版使用手册 — v2.0 · 2026-04-10

1. Overview / 概述

ICS Compass Free is the entry-level edition of the ICS governance evaluation toolkit, designed for first-time users, educators, students, and anyone curious about interstellar civilization governance frameworks.

ICS 星际罗盘 免费版是ICS治理评估工具的入门版本，面向首次用户、教育工作者、学生及所有对星际文明治理框架感兴趣的人。

What You Can Do / 功能一览

  • 3 Core Metrics / 3项核心指标: UCS (Universal Consciousness Spectrum), RFD (Recursive Freedom Degree), CDI (Civilization Development Index)
  • 3 Evaluation Templates / 3个评估模板: Quick AI Governance Check, Technology Impact Screener, Environmental Decision Screener
  • 3 Evaluations/Day / 每日3次评估
  • Simplified Scoring / 简化评分: Slider-based input (0–100 scale)
  • Print Export / 打印导出
  • Bilingual Interface / 双语界面: EN / 中文 (switchable via top-right toggle)

2. Getting Started / 快速开始

Step 1: Choose Language / 选择语言

Click the language toggle (EN/中文) in the top-right corner of the navigation bar. Your preference is saved automatically.

点击导航栏右上角的语言切换按钮（EN/中文），选择偏好将自动保存。

Step 2: Start an Evaluation / 开始评估

Click "Start Evaluation" (开始评估) on the home page, or navigate to the Evaluate page.

Step 3: Choose a Template / 选择模板

The Free edition provides 3 pre-configured templates:

Template模板Best For
Quick AI Governance Check快速AI治理检测Rapid assessment of AI systems against key governance boundaries
Technology Impact Screener技术影响筛查Quick screening of emerging technology societal impacts
Environmental Decision Screener环境决策筛查Evaluate environmental policies through ICS metrics

Step 4: Describe Your Scenario / 描述场景

In the text box, describe the technology, policy, or scenario you want to evaluate. Be specific — the more context you provide, the more meaningful your assessment will be.

在文本框中描述您要评估的技术、政策或场景。描述越具体，评估越有意义。

Step 5: Evaluate Principles & Red Lines / 评估原则与红线

You will be guided through:

  1. 3 Principles (RFP, NRP, REV) — rate compliance level for each
  2. 5 Red Lines (FRL-1 through FRL-5) — check whether each is violated
  3. 3 Metrics (UCS, RFD, CDI) — use sliders to input scores (0–100)

Step 6: View Results / 查看结果

The Results page displays:

  • Radar chart of your 3 metric scores
  • Overall composite score and letter grade (A–F)
  • Principle compliance badges
  • Red line violation warnings

7. FAQ / 常见问题

Q: Is my data stored anywhere? / 数据存储在哪里？

No. All evaluations run entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Your evaluations are stored in browser local storage only.

不会。所有评估完全在浏览器中运行，不向任何服务器发送数据。评估记录仅存储在浏览器本地存储中。

Q: What are the 3 metrics based on? / 3项指标的理论基础是什么？

All metrics are derived from the ICS theoretical framework as published in "The Normative Foundations of Interstellar Civilization: New Three Views and Governance Under Deep Uncertainty" (4 volumes). The Free edition uses simplified versions; full mathematical formulations are available in the Academic edition.

Q: Can I share my results? / 可以分享评估结果吗？

Yes — use the Print function (Ctrl+P) to save as PDF, or take a screenshot of the results page.
