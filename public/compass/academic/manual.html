3. Sensitivity Analysis / 敏感性分析

Sensitivity analysis tests how robust your evaluation results are to changes in weight assumptions.

How It Works / 工作原理

  1. Complete a standard evaluation with your chosen weights
  2. Click "Run Sensitivity Analysis"
  3. Specify the perturbation range (default: ±20%)
  4. The system recalculates your evaluation across all weight combinations within that range
  5. Results show: min score, max score, mean, standard deviation for each metric

Interpretation: A narrow range (small σ) means your result is robust — weight choices don't significantly affect the conclusion. A wide range (large σ) means the result is weight-sensitive and should be reported with caveats.

9. FAQ / 常见问题

Q: Can I publish results from ICS Compass Academic? / 可以发表使用学术版生成的结果吗？

Yes. Please cite the ICS framework using the auto-generated BibTeX entry. The LaTeX export includes a pre-formatted citation block.

Q: How is the stress test robustness score (0.58) calculated? / 压力测试稳健性分数(0.58)如何计算？

It is the framework's own self-assessment: the weighted average of component robustness scores across all 4 stress scenarios. A score of 0.58 indicates "moderate robustness with known vulnerabilities" — the framework is transparent about its own limitations.

Q: Can I add custom stress test scenarios? / 可以添加自定义压力测试场景吗？

Yes. Use the "Custom Stress Test" template to define your own scenarios with custom parameters. Results are compared against the framework's baseline scenarios.
