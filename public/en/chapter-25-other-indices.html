III. Discussion of Completeness

Open Problem 25.1: Is the CDI formal system complete?

At present we cannot prove the completeness of the CDI formal system. Completeness requires that all true propositions about CDI be derivable from the axiom system.

Possible sources of incompleteness:

1. The arbitrariness of the choice of dimensions: why these five dimensions and not others?

2. The subjectivity of weight determination: the choice of the weight function lacks an objective standard

3. The multiplicity of composite functions: the weighted average is only one of many possible ways of combining

Directions for future research:

  • To explore the axiomatic completeness of CDI
  • To study the properties of different composite functions
  • To establish an objective method for determining weights

Limitation One: The Theoretical Insufficiency of the Choice of Dimensions

Why these five dimensions and not others? Formalization itself cannot answer this question. The choice of dimensions depends on the substantive arguments of Volume I; formalization can proceed only on the premise that the dimensions have been given.

  • 20 formal-language definitions
  • 43 axioms
  • 43 theorems and their rigorous proofs
  • Thousands of lines of machine-verification code

This formalization work not only provides a theoretical foundation for the index applications of Volume III, but more importantly, through the process of formalization itself, we have revealed three important new insights:

1. The measurability of the cosmic scale

2. Reversibility as a core dimension of risk

3. The emergence of the index system

These insights prove that formalization is not merely “decoration” but a method capable of producing substantive theoretical contributions.
