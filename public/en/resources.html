Resource Center

This page compiles the core glossary, recommended readings, and related resources of Interstellar Civilization Studies, providing reference for in-depth understanding of ICS.

Core Glossary

Discipline Name and Basic Concepts

English Abbreviation Definition
Interstellar Civilization Studies ICS An interdisciplinary academic field studying the existence, development, governance, and normative issues of intelligent life on a cosmic scale
Deep Time — Time scales beyond human intuitive grasp, typically referring to thousands of years or more
Deep Uncertainty — A cognitive state where outcomes, probabilities, and utilities are all indeterminate

New Three Views Related Terms

English Abbreviation Definition
New Three Views NTV Collective term for New Cosmology, New Life View, and New Cognition View
New Cosmology NC An ontology asserting that the universe is relational, processual, and participatory
New Life View NL A view of life asserting that life is substrate-independent, negentropic, and gradational
New Cognition View NK An epistemology asserting that cognition is participatory, social, and faces deep uncertainty

Normative Principles Related Terms

English Abbreviation Definition
Cosmic Responsibility Ethics CRE The normative framework of Interstellar Civilization Studies
Recursive Freedom Principle RFP The exercise of freedom must not systematically undermine the conditions of freedom for others
Negentropy Responsibility Principle NRP Responsibility to protect and enhance the cosmic stock of negentropy
Reversibility Principle REV Prioritize reversible actions
Forbidden Red Lines FRL Absolute prohibitions that must not be crossed under any circumstances

Indicator System Related Terms

English Abbreviation Definition
Universal Consciousness Scale UCS An indicator measuring the developmental level of consciousness systems
Recursive Freedom Degree RFD An indicator measuring impact on possibility space
Commitment Reversibility & Verifiability CRV An indicator measuring the credibility of commitments
Civilization Development Index CDI An indicator measuring the comprehensive developmental level of civilizations
Memetic Biosafety Containment Level MBCL An indicator assessing the safety level of informational entities
Cosmic-Scale Impact Assessment CSIA An indicator assessing cosmic-scale impacts
