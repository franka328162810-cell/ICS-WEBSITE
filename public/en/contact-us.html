Contact Us

Welcome to connect with the Interstellar Civilization Studies team. Whether you are a researcher interested in our theoretical framework, an institution seeking collaboration, or a thinker concerned about humanity's long-term future, we look forward to hearing from you.

Contact Methods

Academic Collaboration
For academic collaboration, joint research, or lecture invitations, please email us with detailed information about your collaboration intentions and project background.
Media Inquiries
For media interviews, commentary requests, or content licensing, please send an email specifying your requirements and deadlines.

Inquiry Types

We welcome the following types of inquiries:

  • Academic Research: Academic discussions on ICS theoretical frameworks, methodologies, or specific applications
  • Policy Consultation: Policy advisory needs in AI governance, space governance, existential risk management, and related fields
  • Education & Training: Educational collaboration in curriculum development, textbook writing, and faculty training
  • Institutional Partnerships: Collaboration intentions from research institutions, universities, think tanks, and international organizations
  • Corporate Consulting: Consulting needs for technology companies in long-term impact assessment and ethical review
  • Media Collaboration: Media-related matters including interviews, columns, and content licensing
  • Public Engagement: Lecture invitations, science communication activities, and community building

Response Time

To better serve every contact, we commit to:

  • General Inquiries: Response within 48 hours
  • Urgent Matters: Please mark "URGENT" in the email subject line, and we will prioritize your request
  • Complex Projects: For collaboration projects requiring in-depth evaluation, we will provide initial feedback within 7 business days

If you do not receive a reply within the stated timeframe, your email may have been mistakenly filtered as spam. Please try resending or check your spam folder.

Email Writing Guidelines

To help us understand your needs more quickly and accurately, we recommend including the following information in your email:

  • Clear Subject Line: Briefly state the purpose of your email (e.g., "Academic Collaboration Inquiry", "Policy Advisory Request")
  • Self-Introduction: Your name, affiliated institution, position, or identity
  • Specific Needs: Detailed description of your inquiry, collaboration intentions, or questions
  • Background Information: Relevant project background, timeline, expected outcomes, etc.
  • Contact Information: Additional contact methods besides email (optional)

Tip: If your inquiry involves technical details or requires reference materials, please feel free to attach relevant documents to your email. We value every thoughtful exchange.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I visit your office directly?

A: We primarily communicate via email. If an in-person meeting is needed, please schedule in advance via email.

Q: Do you provide paid consulting services?

A: We provide free consultation to academic institutions, government agencies, and non-profit organizations. For commercial consulting services, please inquire via email.

Q: I'm a student. Can I apply for internship or research opportunities?

A: We welcome outstanding students to join us. Please send your resume, research interests statement, and advisor recommendation letter to our email.

Q: How can I obtain the "Interstellar Civilization Studies" publications?

A: Please visit our Publications page for purchase channels and digital download information.

Q: Do you accept donations or funding?

A: We welcome funding that supports the development of Interstellar Civilization Studies. Please contact us via email to discuss specifics.
