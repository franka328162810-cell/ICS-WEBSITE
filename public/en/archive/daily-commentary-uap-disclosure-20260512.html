The Illusion of Transparency: An ICS Reading of the Pentagon UAP Disclosure

📅 May 12, 2026 | ⏱️ Reading Time: ~20 min | 🏷️SETI · Existential Risk · Cognitive Governance

On May 8, 2026, the U.S. government released over 160 declassified UAP documents under the PURSUE Plan—the most extensive government transparency action on anomalous phenomena in history. Yet the public frenzy that followed reveals less about the cosmos than it does about us. This commentary applies the ICS framework—specifically the New Three Worldviews (NC, NL, NK) and the three core principles (RFP, NRP, REV)—to examine what was truly disclosed, and what was quietly concealed.
