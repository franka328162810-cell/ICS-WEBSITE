Dialogue with AI Safety Research: Technical and Normative Complementarity

AI safety research focuses on ensuring artificial intelligence systems are safe, controllable, and beneficial. This field has developed rich technical solutions but is relatively weak in normative frameworks. ICS and AI safety research form a complementary relationship—AI safety provides technical details, ICS provides normative frameworks.

What is AI Safety Research?

Core Problems

Core problems addressed by AI safety research:

Problem Content
Alignment Problem How to align AI goals with human values?
Control Problem How to maintain control over AI systems?
Robustness How to ensure AI operates safely in various scenarios?
Interpretability How to understand AI decision-making processes?
Scaling Risks How to address new risks from AI capability improvements?

ICS's Critical Observations

Problems with the "Alignment" Concept

Problem Content
Align to What? What are "human values"? Whose values? Which values?
Value Heterogeneity Human values themselves are heterogeneous, cannot be simply aggregated
Value Evolution Human values evolve, what is the target of alignment?
Control Assumption "Alignment" implies human control of AI, is this reasonable?

Problems with Instrumentalist Assumptions

Problem Content
Subject Possibility If AI develops consciousness, is instrumentalism appropriate?
Rights Question Should we consider AI "rights" or "interests"?
Coexistence Question How to shift from "control" paradigm to "coexistence" paradigm?

Recursive Freedom Replaces Alignment

ICS suggests replacing "human value alignment" with "recursive freedom protection":

Alignment Framework Recursive Freedom Framework
AI should align with human values AI development should not systematically undermine subjects' freedom conditions
Question: What are human values? Answer: Protect possibility space
Question: Whose values? Answer: Freedom of all subjects (including AI)
