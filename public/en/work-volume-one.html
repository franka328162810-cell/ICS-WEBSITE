Volume I: Theoretical Foundations
Reconstructing Civilizational Norms from a Cosmic Perspective

Volume I of Normative Foundations of Interstellar Civilization is the theoretical cornerstone of the entire four-volume work, systematically constructing a normative theoretical framework oriented toward deep time scales and diverse intelligent agents. This volume proposes the "New Three Views" (New Cosmology, New Biology, New Epistemology) as the ontological-epistemological foundation, from which three core normative principles are derived, providing a solid philosophical foundation for interstellar civilization governance.

Core Challenges

This volume begins by directly confronting the fundamental challenges facing human civilization: When our technological capabilities have reached interstellar scales, when artificial intelligence may surpass human intelligence within this century, when our decisions may have consequences lasting thousands of years or more, how do we establish a normative framework applicable to such vast spatiotemporal scales?

Traditional ethics, based on assumptions of anthropocentrism, terrestrial scale, and short-term horizons, appears inadequate when facing issues such as AGI governance, extraterrestrial contact, and intergenerational justice.

Three core puzzles: What is the cosmic foundation of normativity? How to maintain rationality under deep uncertainty? How to attribute long-range responsibility?

Content Structure

Part I: Problems and Methods

Explicitly presents three core puzzles that constitute the starting point of this work. The author candidly declares methodological commitments and boundaries, adopting an "engineering narrative" style that combines philosophical argumentation with formalized expression, maintaining both theoretical rigor and operational feasibility.

Part II: The New Three Views

The core innovation of this volume, providing theoretical support for cross-civilizational interaction, AI ethics, and extraterrestrial life ethics:

  • New Cosmology (NC) — Based on process philosophy and quantum mechanics, argues that the fundamental pattern of the universe is relations rather than entities, existence is becoming rather than static being, and observers are participants rather than spectators
  • New Biology (NL) — Breaks through the limitations of carbon-based life, proposes the possibility of substrate-independent life, defines life as negentropy organization, and views consciousness as a continuous spectrum rather than binary classification
  • New Epistemology (NK) — Integrates 4E cognitive science (embodied, embedded, extended, enactive), emphasizes the participatory, social, and situated nature of cognition, and acknowledges the ineliminability of deep uncertainty

Part III: Normative Principles

Derives three core principles from the New Three Views, integrated into the "Cosmic Responsibility Ethics" framework:

  • Recursive Freedom Principle (RFP) — Requires that any agent exercising freedom must protect the possibility of other agents exercising equivalent freedom, unifying freedom and responsibility
  • Negentropy Responsibility Principle (NRP) — Based on the second law of thermodynamics, requires intelligent agents to bear responsibility for maintaining and increasing cosmic negentropy, providing physical anchoring for environmental ethics and intergenerational justice
  • Reversibility Principle (REV) — Requires prioritizing reversible action paths under deep uncertainty, preserving future choice space

Core Features

Revisability Design

This volume particularly emphasizes revisability. The author explicitly declares this as a "first-generation draft model," adopting semantic versioning (v1.0.0), establishing rollback mechanisms, and inviting the academic community to critique and refine. This open attitude reflects the author's clear awareness of theoretical limitations and commitment to academic integrity.

Engineering Narrative

Combines philosophical argumentation with formalized expression, featuring extensive diagrams, formalized definitions, and argumentation nodes, maintaining both theoretical rigor and operational feasibility.

Interdisciplinary Integration

Integrates cutting-edge achievements from multiple disciplines including process philosophy, quantum mechanics, 4E cognitive science, and thermodynamics, providing a solid scientific foundation for interstellar civilization governance.
