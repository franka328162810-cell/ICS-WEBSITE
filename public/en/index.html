What is ICS?

Interstellar Civilization Studies (ICS) is an interdisciplinary academic field based on the "New Three Views" (New Cosmology, New Life View, New Epistemology) as its ontological and epistemological foundation, with cosmic responsibility ethics as its normative framework.

It studies the existence, development, governance, and normative issues of intelligent life on a cosmic scale. Its core mission is to provide systematic theoretical guidance and practical solutions for humanity's long-term survival, interstellar expansion, and potential cross-civilization contact.
