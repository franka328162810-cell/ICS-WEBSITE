Mission

The mission of Interstellar Civilization Studies is:

Under conditions of deep uncertainty, to provide normative guidance for the long-term flourishing of intelligent life in the universe, protect and expand the possibility space of future agents, promote peaceful coexistence of diverse forms of intelligence, and provide theoretical resources and practical solutions for major governance challenges facing contemporary human civilization.

Summarized in a maxim:

"Freedom is the guardianship of possibility"
