About ICS

Interstellar Civilization Studies is an interdisciplinary academic field focused on cosmic-scale research. It addresses not only the distant interstellar future but also provides normative frameworks for contemporary urgent issues such as AI governance, space exploration, and existential risks. This page systematically introduces the definition, background, and research scope of this emerging discipline.

Discipline Definition

Core Definition

Interstellar Civilization Studies (ICS) is an interdisciplinary academic field that studies the existence, development, governance, and normative issues of intelligent life on a cosmic scale.

It is grounded in the "New Three Views" (New Cosmology, New Biology, and New Epistemology) as its theoretical foundation, employs "Cosmic Responsibility Ethics" as its normative framework, and uses the "Protocol Stack Methodology" as its institutional design tool. It aims to provide systematic theoretical guidance and practical solutions for the long-term survival of human civilization, interstellar expansion, and possible inter-civilizational contact.

English Name Explanation

"Interstellar Civilization Studies" as the standard English name follows academic naming conventions:

- Interstellar: From Latin inter (between) + stellar (of the stars)

- Civilization: From Latin civilis (relating to citizens)

- Studies: Indicates this is an interdisciplinary research field, not a single discipline

Background

Technological Context

Humanity stands at a critical juncture in technological development:

- Artificial intelligence is rapidly developing and may achieve Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) within this century

- Space technology makes interplanetary travel a reality, with increasing possibilities for interstellar travel

- Biotechnology brings capabilities to engineer life, including gene editing and synthetic biology

- Information technology creates a globally interconnected information space, profoundly transforming human cognition and social organization

These technological developments bring not only unprecedented opportunities but also existential risks—risks that could lead to the termination or permanent decline of human civilization.

Cognitive Context

Human cognitive capabilities face profound challenges:

- Deep Uncertainty: Many critical decisions involve situations where outcomes, probabilities, and utilities are all uncertain

- Temporal Scale Mismatch: Cognitive abilities formed through human evolution struggle to grasp time scales beyond several decades

- Complexity Explosion: The systemic complexity brought by globalization and technological development exceeds the capacity of traditional analytical methods

Governance Context

Existing governance systems face fundamental challenges:

- International System: Governance systems based on nation-states struggle to address global and intergenerational issues

- Time Horizon: There exists fundamental tension between the short-term nature of political cycles and the long-term impact of technology

- Agent Expansion: There is a lack of theoretical resources for incorporating AI and possible extraterrestrial intelligence into governance frameworks

Core Research Areas

- Foundational Theory Research: Philosophical justification of the New Three Views, normative derivation of Cosmic Responsibility Ethics

- Applied Research: AI governance frameworks, space governance schemes, existential risk assessment

- Institutional Design: Protocol Stack Methodology, Multi-Intelligence Tribunal, intergenerational trust mechanisms

- Indicator Systems: Cosmic Consciousness Scale (UCS), Recursive Freedom Degree (RFD), and other six major indicators

Mission

The mission of Interstellar Civilization Studies is:

Under conditions of deep uncertainty, to provide normative guidance for the long-term flourishing of intelligent life in the universe, protect and expand the possibility space of future agents, promote peaceful coexistence of diverse forms of intelligence, and provide theoretical resources and practical solutions for major governance challenges facing contemporary human civilization.

Summarized in a maxim:

"Freedom is the guardianship of possibility"
