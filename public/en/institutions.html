Institutional Design: Protocol Stack Methodology

Interstellar Civilization Studies has developed a unique institutional design methodology—the Protocol Stack Methodology. It borrows from the layered architecture of computer network protocols, dividing the governance system into five layers, maintaining stability of foundational principles while allowing flexible evolution at the operational level, particularly suited for addressing deep uncertainty and long-timescale governance challenges.

Protocol Stack Methodology Overview

What is the Protocol Stack Methodology?

The Protocol Stack Methodology borrows from the layered architecture thinking of computer network protocols (such as TCP/IP), designing the governance system as a multi-layered structure:

  • Different layers have different stability requirements
  • Higher layers depend on lower layers, lower layers are independent of higher layers
  • Supports layered evolution and version management

Why Do We Need a Protocol Stack?

Challenges faced by traditional governance systems:

Challenge Traditional Problem Protocol Stack Solution
Rigidity Difficult to adapt to change Layered flexibility
Fragility Local changes affect the whole Layered decoupling
Short-termism Difficult to sustain across generations Versioned evolution
Irreversibility Errors difficult to correct Rollback mechanism

Core Institutional Designs

Multi-Intelligence Adjudication Court (MIAC)

Function: Adjudication institution for disputes between cross-substrate entities

Features:

  • Accommodates representatives of humans, AI, and potential extraterrestrial intelligence
  • Adjudicates based on normative principles
  • Decisions are binding

Cosmic-Scale Institutional Review Board (CIRB)

Function: Ethical review institution for major actions

Features:

  • Reviews actions with cosmic-scale impact
  • Assesses based on CSIA metrics
  • Has authority to veto or require modifications

Intergenerational Trust (IGT)

Function: Institutional arrangement representing the interests of future entities

Features:

  • Establishes "agents" for future entities
  • Protects long-term interests from being sacrificed to short-term interests
  • Manages intergenerational resources and commitments

Versioned Governance and Rollback

Versioned Governance

The protocol stack supports versioned management of institutions:

  • Each revision produces a new version
  • Version history is retained
  • Clear version numbers and change logs

Rollback Mechanism

When necessary, it is possible to roll back to a previous version:

  • Clear rollback conditions and procedures
  • State information required for rollback is retained
  • Transition arrangements after rollback

Backward Compatibility

Higher-layer revisions must not violate lower-layer constraints:

  • L₄ revisions must not violate L₃ framework
  • L₃ revisions must not violate L₂ principles
  • And so on

Methodology Characteristics
