Existential Risk Management: Safeguarding Civilization's Future

Existential risks are threats that could lead to human extinction or permanent civilizational decline. Nuclear war, misaligned superintelligence, engineered pandemics, climate collapse—these threats could end the human story. Interstellar Civilization Studies provides forbidden red lines and deep uncertainty decision frameworks for existential risk management.

What are Existential Risks?

Definition

Existential Risk (X-risk) refers to risks that could lead to the following outcomes:

  • Human Extinction: The complete disappearance of Homo sapiens
  • Civilizational Collapse: Irreversible collapse of civilization
  • Permanent Decline: Permanent and substantial reduction of human potential

Unique Characteristics of Existential Risks

Existential risks differ fundamentally from ordinary risks:

Characteristic Ordinary Risk Existential Risk
Occurrence Can occur multiple times Can only occur once—no second chance
Learning Opportunity Can learn from failure No learning opportunity
Impact Scope Local or recoverable Global and irreversible
Probability Assessment Usually estimable Deep uncertainty

ICS Existential Risk Framework

Existential Risks and Forbidden Red Lines

Existential risks are directly linked to forbidden red lines:

Forbidden Red Line Relationship to Existential Risk
FRL-1 Global totalitarianism constitutes systematic violation of consciousness sovereignty
FRL-2 Nuclear war and climate collapse cause massive depletion of cosmic negentropy
FRL-3 Any realization of existential risk will systematically contract future possibility space

Core Insight:

The realization of existential risk equals the complete deprivation of freedom conditions for all potential future agents. This is the most extreme violation of the Recursive Freedom Principle.

Application of Normative Principles

Principle Application in Existential Risk Management
Recursive Freedom Principle (RFP) Avoid any action that could lead to the zeroing of future agents' possibility space
Negentropy Responsibility Principle (NRP) Protect human civilization as an important carrier of cosmic negentropy
Reversibility Principle (REV) In existential risk domains, prioritizing reversible paths is crucial
Forbidden Red Lines (FRL) Existential risk domains have the strictest application of forbidden red lines

Deep Uncertainty and Decision-Making

Challenges of Deep Uncertainty

The existential risk domain faces deep uncertainty:

Dimension Content
Outcome Uncertainty Specific consequences of existential risks are difficult to fully foresee
Probability Uncertainty Probabilities of most existential risks cannot be reliably estimated
Model Uncertainty Our risk models may have fundamental flaws
Value Uncertainty How to balance contemporary and future interests is contested

ICS Decision Framework

Facing deep uncertainty, ICS provides the following decision principles:

Principle Content
Reversibility Priority Prioritize reversible actions, preserving error-correction possibilities
Maximize Survival Make long-term survival of human civilization the highest priority
Diversity Protection Protect option diversity, avoid single points of failure
Bottom-Line Thinking Avoid worst-case scenarios rather than pursue optimal outcomes
Precautionary Principle Remain vigilant about potential existential risks when evidence is insufficient

Existential Risk Decision Matrix

Scenario Decision Guidance
High Probability × High Impact Highest priority response, invest substantial resources
Low Probability × High Impact (Existential Risk) High attention, should not be ignored due to low probability
High Probability × Low Impact Routine risk management
Low Probability × Low Impact Low priority

Recursive Freedom Degree (RFD) Application in Existential Risk

Core Assessment

For existential risk-related decisions, RFD assessment is crucial:

Existential Risk ⇔ RFD→0

Any action that could cause RFD to approach zero constitutes an existential risk.

RFD Assessment of Technology Development Paths

Technology Path RFD Assessment Recommendation
Aligned AGI RFD > 1 (Expands possibilities) Support, but requires continuous monitoring
Misaligned AGI RFD → 0 (Existential risk) Must be avoided
Controlled Nuclear Fusion RFD > 1 (Clean energy) Actively support
Offensive Biotechnology RFD → 0 (Pandemic risk) Strictly prohibit

Role of Cosmic-Scale IRB in Existential Risk

The Cosmic-Scale IRB (C-IRB) should conduct special review of the following activities:

  • Research that could create human-level or superhuman AI
  • Research that could enhance pathogen dangerousness
  • Activities that could cause global climate system tipping points
  • Other activities that could touch forbidden red lines
