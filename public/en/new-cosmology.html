New Cosmology: Relational, Processual, Participatory

New Cosmology proposes that the universe is not a static container composed of isolated entities, but a dynamic process constituted by relations, continuously evolving, with observers participating within it. This ontological stance is based on the latest interpretations of quantum mechanics and has profound implications for our understanding of life and intelligence in the cosmos.

Core Propositions

New Cosmology comprises three interrelated core propositions:

Proposition NC-1: Relationality

The fundamental mode of existence in the universe is relations, not isolated entities.

Traditional views hold that the universe is composed of independently existing "things," with relations being derivative. New Cosmology inverts this priority: relations are fundamental, and entities are nodes within relations.

Formal Expression
∀x : ∃R, y : x = Node(R, y)
(Any entity x is a node in some relation R relative to other entities y)

Proposition NC-2: Processuality

The fundamental mode of existence in the universe is process, not static states.

The universe is not a static "being" but a dynamic "becoming." Reality is not discovered but continuously emerges.

Formal Expression
Reality = lim Process(t)
t→∞
(Reality is the limit of infinite process)

Proposition NC-3: Participation

Observers are participants in cosmic processes, not spectators.

Observation is not passive mirroring but active participatory construction. Observers participate in the emergence of reality through the act of observation itself.

Formal Expression
Reality(t) = f(Universe(t), Observer(t))
(Reality is a function of both universe state and observer)

Scientific Foundations

Insights from Quantum Mechanics

The most important scientific foundation of New Cosmology comes from quantum mechanics, particularly Relational Quantum Mechanics (RQM):

  • Measurement Problem: Quantum systems only exhibit definite properties when interacting with other systems
  • Entanglement Phenomenon: Quantum entanglement demonstrates that relations between particles are more fundamental than the particles themselves
  • Observer Role: Observers are not external to quantum systems but are part of the quantum universe

"Quantum mechanics is a theory about how the world appears relative to physical systems, not about how the world is in itself."

— Carlo Rovelli

Support from Process Philosophy

New Cosmology is also supported by Whitehead's process philosophy:

  • Actual entities are processes, not substances
  • Relations constitute the fundamental structure of reality
  • Creativity is the ultimate category of the universe

Philosophical Implications

Reconstruction of Realism

New Cosmology is neither anti-realism nor relativism, but a form of relational realism:

  • Reality exists, but its mode of existence is relational
  • Objectivity is not absolute but inter-subjective
  • Knowledge is possible, but knowledge is participatory rather than mirror-like

Transcending Subject-Object Dichotomy

New Cosmology transcends the traditional subject-object dichotomy:

Traditional View New Cosmology
Subject and object are separate Subject and object are nodes in relations
Observation is passive mirroring Observation is active participation
Reality is independent of observers Reality emerges through observation

Implications for Normativity

New Cosmology has important implications for normative thinking:

Relationality of Responsibility

If existence is relational, then responsibility is also relational. We have responsibilities to others because we are in relations with others.

Participation in Action

If we are participants in cosmic processes, then every action we take shapes the future of the universe. This endows action with deeper meaning and responsibility.

Toward Cosmic Responsibility Ethics

New Cosmology provides the ontological foundation for Cosmic Responsibility Ethics:

Because we are in relations with the cosmos, because we are participants in cosmic processes, we have responsibility for the future of the universe.

Core Maxim

"Quantum is not just metaphor — it is computational structure"

This maxim reminds us: New Cosmology does not treat quantum mechanics as a vague metaphor, but takes seriously its profound ontological implications.
