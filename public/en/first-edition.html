The First Edition (2026)

The Normative Foundations of Interstellar Civilization First Edition · 2026 · v1.0.0.0

New Three Views and Governance under Deep Uncertainty. The four-volume first edition, approximately 2.56 million characters, is the founding text of Interstellar Civilization Studies. It proposed the New Three Views as the theoretical foundation, derived three core norms, and established the forbidden red lines as absolute constraints.

The first edition is retained in full as part of the discipline’s documented record. It has been superseded as the current edition by the six-volume second edition, The Normative Reconstruction of Interstellar Civilization (2027 revision), but remains citable and publicly archived.

Zenodo Preprint Record

The first edition was released as a preprint on the Zenodo preprint platform. This is the authoritative, citable record for the 2026 edition; please cite it when referring to the first-edition text.

A surgical revision, not a demolition

The second edition preserves more than 80% of the first edition’s valid content and reaches the same core conclusions. What changes is the rigour of the argumentative path: five structural weaknesses identified in the first edition — argumentative leaps, decorative formalism, circular co-construction, weak measurement theory, and technical disconnection — are each repaired and explicitly documented. Every revision is annotated with its rationale and its correspondence to the first edition, so the two editions can be read in parallel.

The current edition. The six-volume second edition is complete as a manuscript. Its publication route and specific publication date will be confirmed and announced in 2027.
