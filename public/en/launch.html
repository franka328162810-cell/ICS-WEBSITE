01 · Why this exists

The news tells you what happened.
We tell you what it means — and what to do about it.

Every day, thousands of papers, filings and press releases cross your desk. The signal is buried. The noise is loud. The one question that matters goes unanswered.

Ordinary coverage

Reports the event

  • What happened, in the order it happened
  • Quotes from the loudest participants
  • Numbers without a normative frame
  • No verdict — because a verdict is risky
  • Yesterday's urgency, forgotten tomorrow
  • Written for the widest possible audience
The Stewardship Brief

Judges the event

  • What it means for civilization — stated plainly
  • A red-line check before anything else
  • Three principles and six metrics, applied
  • An explicit verdict, with its uncertainty labelled
  • A 50-year and 100-year+ lens (T2 / T3)
  • Written for the people who must act
One frontier signal. Five-part framework. One auditable judgment — every single weekday.
02 · One minute to read

One minute to the verdict — or read on to the audit desk

Every issue opens on the same five-part structure. Read those five and you already hold the day's most important judgement, plus the checkpoint that will settle it. Everything below is there for whoever wants to go deeper.

①
The Verdict
今日判断
One sentence, and the reservation stated up front — the conditions under which the judgement fails, named before anything else.
②
Three Lines
三条理由
No build-up. Three lines only: the three that carry the verdict, and the one it cannot reach.
③
So What
所以呢
What the judgement changes about what others may ask. Its whole force and its whole limit, in one line.
④
The Biggest Unknown
当前最大未知
What we do not know gets its own section, with the confidence lean stated — speculation is never passed off as a verdict.
⑤
One Thing to Watch Next
下一步观察点
A date, an ID and a test standard. The review outcome is published in Following once due — including the falsified ones.

One minute to the conclusion, one minute to what to watch and when it gets tested — the rest of the issue reads on demand.

04 · A full issue

A full issue, start to finish

THE STEWARDSHIP BRIEF · NO.003 · 2026.09.20 · PREVIEW · UTC+8
California installed no kill switch; it wrote "can this be independently verified" into a dated to-do list
The Stewardship Brief · 今日文明简报 · The Verdict
§ 01 · THE VERDICT

On 18 September, Governor Newsom signed Executive Order N-9-26: it shuts down no company and builds no kill switch. It only requires California agencies to submit, by 16 November, proposals for how outside bodies could independently verify what AI labs claim. This is the first time a government has set a deadline for its own inability to verify. The reservation sits at the front: the judgement fails if the 16 November recommendations leave third-party access voluntary, or fail to define an operable standard for a qualified audit body.

§ 02 · THREE LINES · RED LINE / THREE PRINCIPLES / METRICS

One. The order names four items — third-party access, reproducible testing, kill-switch re-verification, runaway definition — letting outsiders check a lab's claims without its permission. Two. "Safety" thereby turns from marketing language into a compliance asset with real cost: stateable, and checkable. Three. It cannot reach the question of recursive self-improvement. ICS grading: the red-line check finds three approached, FRL-5 closest (deploying non-shutdown-capable systems); of the three principles, RFP compressed, NRP externalised, REV not rollback-able today — REV is the tightest. All qualitative; no numeric score is published.

§ 03 · IMPLICATIONS

For governance (reader group I): to bind labs that decline voluntary commitments, three clauses must stand together — fix the accreditation conditions, give loss-of-control events a narrow, observable definition, and reserve a mandatory path for non-participants; drop any one and the other two fail. For investment: "safety" starts being priced as a compliance asset. For research: verifiability turns from a normative claim into an operable observation point. One thing you can do this week: check whether the AI systems your organisation procures come with an independent third-party reliability verification report; if not, write it into your next procurement conditions.

05 · The method

How a verdict is made

Our verdicts proceed in three steps: each fact is graded A–D by source type, the six indicators then yield a qualitative judgement, and every conclusion carries a falsification condition. The five items below are the tooling behind those steps.

1
FRL
Red-line check
The forbidden red lines first: whether each is "approached" or "touched", and why the distinction matters.
2
RFP · NRP · REV
Three principles
Is the possibility space compressed / is entropy cost externalised / can it still be rolled back.
3
Six indicators · qualitative
Indicator judgement
The direction in which the possibility space is moving. No numeric score is published — the indicators are qualitative, not measured values.
4
T2 · T3 · deep time
Time scales
T2 (50 years) and T3 (100 years and beyond) are scenario windows marking how long a choice stays reversible; "deep time" is a geological scale (beyond 10,000 years) — a statement of value orientation making no quantitative promise. The three must not be conflated.
5
Falsification
Falsify, then review
Every conclusion carries a falsification condition with an ID, a confidence level and a review date; results are published once due.

Each fact is graded A–D by source type, the qualitative judgement follows, and the falsification condition closes it — three steps, or there is no verdict. Built on six volumes · 4.68M characters · 27 formal systems · ~180 theorems · 120 machine-verified proofs. Every judgment is traceable to the source text. Nothing is invented.

09 · Questions

Before you join

What exactly do I get that free readers don't?
Free readers get the weekly public recap (a simplified version of the previous week's focus, deliberately one week delayed) and the full in-depth research column. Members get the real-time daily edition, Monday to Friday: the complete daily focus plus the 11-domain scan, delivered to their inbox on the day of publication. The one-week time gap is not a limitation — it is the product.
Is this just another AI-generated newsletter?
No. The framework is a six-volume normative corpus with ~180 theorems, 27 formal systems and 120 machine-verified proofs. AI assists with collection and drafting; the judgment, calibration and final verdict are the author's. Every claim carries an evidence grade from [A] traceable public record to [D] unverified rumour — so you can audit the reasoning, not just trust the voice.
How is this different from Stratechery or an analyst note?
Stratechery reads technology through a business-strategy lens. ICS reads it through a normative civilizational frame — red lines, three principles, six metrics and deep time. Where a business analyst asks "is this a good investment?", the ICS framework also asks "should this exist, who pays its entropy cost, and can we reverse it?" You get both the strategic and the normative read.
Do I pay anything to join now?
Joining the launch list now is completely free — you only leave an email. When the site goes live on 10.23, the founding-member early-bird rate opens ($99/yr, from $150; $15/mo), and you'll receive the priority activation link so you can decide then whether to join.
What does a member get that a free reader doesn't?
Free readers receive the weekly public recap (simplified, one week delayed) and the full in-depth research column. Members get four additional real-time layers: The Stewardship Brief (full weekday edition), the weekly synthesis (review + outlook), the monthly deep dive (5,000-word report) and the quarterly six-metric assessment. Academic content stays free for everyone — membership never changes that.
Do you publish numeric scores?
No. The six metrics are qualitative judgments, not measured values, so no numeric score is published. Instead every verdict runs in three steps: each fact is first graded A–D by source type, the direction in which the possibility space is moving is then judged, and every conclusion carries a stated falsification condition. Each judgment is numbered with a review date, and the review outcome — including falsified ones — is published in the Following column once due.
What if I cancel?
30-day money-back guarantee. Read a full month; if it isn't worth it, reply to any email and we refund in full, no questions asked. Monthly members can cancel anytime. Founding annual members keep the early-bird rate for as long as their subscription stays active.
Will this be in English, Chinese, or both?
Both editions exist. You are reading the English page, so your launch notices and public recap will be in English. If you'd rather receive the Chinese editions, use the 中文 switch in the top navigation — subscriptions are recorded by the language of the page you join from, so the right edition reaches you from the first email.
