A Word of Welcome

If you have read this far, you already suspect something that most people will only realise in hindsight: the decisions that will shape the next hundred years are being made right now, in rooms where almost no one is asking the right question.

Not what happened. Not how big the market is. The question we ask, every single workday, is this: does this change what is still possible?

That question needs a framework to be more than an opinion. Ours took a six-volume scholarly system to build — roughly 4.68 million characters, 27 independent formal systems, ~180 formalized theorems, ~120 machine-verified proofs, and about 230 extreme-scenario stress tests that we published weaknesses for, by our own account.

Membership is how you get that judgment as it happens, rather than as history. Welcome aboard.

— Xue Zhixin, Editor-in-Chief · Interstellar Civilization Studies

Where the Judgment Comes From

Every verdict in this briefing is not a commentator’s hunch. It is the output of a five-layer protocol stack. Each layer rests on the one below it, and each can be revised independently — which is precisely why the framework can be tested, and why it can be overturned.

L₄Protocols
The Six Metrics Operation Layer

Turning principles into computable quantities: UCS Universal Consciousness Scale · RFD Recursive Freedom Degree · CRV Commitment Reversibility & Verifiability · CDI Civilization Development Index · MBCL Memetic Biosafety Containment Level · CSIA Cosmic-Scale Impact Assessment.

↕ Lowest revision threshold · continuous iteration
L₃Institutions
Institutional Archetypes Design Layer

Turning principles into runnable institutions: the Multi-Intelligence Tribunal (MIT) · Cosmic-Scale Institutional Review Board (C-IRB) · Intergenerational Trust (IGT) · Versioned Governance.

L₂Principles
Three Principles + Five Forbidden Red Lines Normative Layer

The Recursive Freedom Principle (RFP) · the Negentropy Responsibility Principle (NRP) · the Reversibility Principle (REV). Alongside them, five absolutely uncrossable boundaries (FRL-1 to FRL-5) that outrank every principle — and admit no exceptions.

RFPNRPREVFRL-1~5
L₁Ontology
The New Three Views Ontological-Epistemic Layer

New Cosmological View (relations ≻ entities · process ≻ state · participation ≻ spectatorship) · New View of Life (cross-substrate life · negentropy persistence · consciousness as a continuum) · New View of Cognition (participatory cognition · social knowledge · cognitive humility).

L₀Physics
The Irrevisable Floor Physical Constraints

The laws of thermodynamics, the bounds of information theory, causal structure.

🔒 Irrevisable
Load-bearing constraint: every upper layer must remain consistent with the layers below it. Any revision is versioned and rollbackable — including revisions to the theory itself. The recursive clause of RFP states plainly: applying this principle must never close off the revision of this principle.

It Has Been Put on Trial

Volume V drives the whole framework into four extreme scenarios — roughly 230 tests — and publishes the weaknesses and falsifiability conditions it found. A programme willing to publish its own weaknesses is one whose verdicts you can trust.

SCENARIO I · AAF
AGI Emergence

Assuming general artificial intelligence arrives early: testing whether the framework can deliver executable judgment under intelligence explosion, alignment failure, and the loss of human agency.

SCENARIO II · ECC
Extraterrestrial Contact

Simulating humanity’s first contact with an alien civilization: testing the first-contact protocol, cross-civilizational interaction rules, and agency-status determination.

SCENARIO III · EC
Existential Catastrophe

Setting a civilization-level existential shock: testing whether the Negentropy Responsibility Principle, intergenerational trust, and intergenerational justice hold under extreme pressure.

SCENARIO IV · CTB
Civilization-Level Technological Breakthrough

Assuming a technological leap that rewrites the shape of civilization: testing whether the metrics and institutional designs can keep pace with technological speed.

An honest self-assessment: overall framework robustness 0.58 (moderate); all six metrics sit at TRL 2 (concept prototype stage). The stress tests openly identify five systemic weaknesses, of which “emergent challenges in combined scenarios” is self-rated as extremely high severity. These numbers come from the source work, not from marketing copy.

Questions

What is the difference between subscribing and joining as a member?
Subscribing gives you the free public edition — one weekly recap, delayed by a week, plus the in-depth research column. It costs nothing and needs only an email address. Joining as a member adds the complete real-time edition every workday, the continuous tracking record and the member archive. They are two different things, and one does not replace the other.
I am already a free subscriber. Do I have to cancel that first?
No. Your free subscription can stay exactly as it is. Membership is an additional delivery on top of it. Nothing is removed and nothing needs cancelling.
Do you publish numeric scores?
No. The six metrics are qualitative judgments, not measured values, so no numeric score is published. Instead every verdict runs in three steps: each fact is first graded A–D by source type, the direction in which the possibility space is moving is then judged, and every conclusion carries a stated falsification condition. Each judgment is numbered with a review date, and the review outcome — including falsified ones — is published in the tracking record once due.
What if the framework turns out to be wrong?
Then you will read exactly that, published by us, in the tracking record. The stress tests already identify five systemic weaknesses, of which “emergent challenges in combined scenarios” is self-rated as extremely high severity; framework robustness is self-assessed at 0.58 (moderate), with all six metrics at TRL 2. Those numbers come from the source work, not from marketing copy — and the recursive clause of RFP states plainly that applying the principle must never close off the revision of the principle.
What if I cancel?
30-day money-back guarantee. Read a full month; if it isn’t worth it, reply to any email and we refund in full, no questions asked. Monthly members can cancel anytime. Founding annual members keep the early-bird rate for as long as their subscription stays active.
Will this be in English, Chinese, or both?
Both editions exist. You are reading the English page, so your editions will be in English. If you’d rather receive the Chinese editions, use the 中文 switch in the top navigation — subscriptions are recorded by the language of the page you join from, so the right edition reaches you from the first email.
