New Three Views: Theoretical Foundation of ICS

The New Three Views—New Cosmology, New Life View, New Cognition—constitute the ontological and epistemological foundation of Interstellar Civilization Studies. They are not arbitrary philosophical assumptions, but a systematic reconstruction of worldviews based on cutting-edge contemporary scientific achievements, providing a necessary conceptual framework for understanding cosmic-scale problems.

What Are the New Three Views?

The New Three Views (NTV) are the theoretical foundation of Interstellar Civilization Studies, including:

View English Name Abbr. Core Proposition
New Cosmology New Cosmology NC The universe is relational, processual, and participatory
New Life View New Life View NL Life is substrate-independent, negentropy-based, and gradational
New Cognition New Cognition NK Cognition is participatory, social, and faces deep uncertainty

These three views are interconnected and mutually supportive, forming a coherent worldview system.

Why Do We Need the New Three Views?

Traditional worldviews encounter fundamental difficulties when facing cosmic-scale problems:

Dilemma of Traditional Cosmology

- Viewing the universe as a static container composed of isolated entities

- Treating observers as external spectators outside the universe

- Difficulty understanding the relational nature revealed by quantum mechanics

Dilemma of Traditional Life View

- Restricting life to specific forms of carbon-based organisms

- Relying on essentialist dichotomy: living/non-living

- Unable to provide a theoretical position for new entities like AI

Dilemma of Traditional Cognition

- Assuming knowledge is a mirror reflection of the objective world

- Ignoring the participatory and constructive nature of cognition

- Lacking epistemological resources to address deep uncertainty

The New Three Views are a systematic response to these dilemmas.

Scientific Basis of the New Three Views

The New Three Views are not pure philosophical speculation, but are based on cutting-edge achievements of contemporary science:

Scientific Basis of New Cosmology

- Quantum Mechanics: Reveals the relational nature of physical reality

- Relational Quantum Mechanics: Theoretical contributions by Carlo Rovelli and others

- Process Philosophy: Whitehead's process metaphysics

Scientific Basis of New Life View

- Non-equilibrium Thermodynamics: Prigogine's dissipative structure theory

- Systems Biology: Systemic and hierarchical nature of life

- Artificial Life Research: Substrate independence of life

Scientific Basis of New Cognition

- Embodied Cognitive Science: Bodily and situational nature of cognition

- Social Epistemology: Social construction of knowledge

- Decision Science: Decision theory under deep uncertainty

Core Propositions of the New Three Views

Three Propositions of New Cosmology

Proposition Content
NC-1 Relationality The fundamental mode of existence in the universe is relations, not isolated entities
NC-2 Processuality The fundamental mode of existence in the universe is process, not static states
NC-3 Participation Observers are participants in cosmic processes, not spectators

Three Propositions of New Life View

Proposition Content
NL-1 Substrate Independence Life does not depend on specific material substrates
NL-2 Negentropy Essence Life is essentially the maintenance and growth of local negentropy
NL-3 Gradation Life is gradational rather than binary

Three Propositions of New Cognition

Proposition Content
NK-1 Participatory Cognition Cognition is not mirroring, but participatory construction
NK-2 Social Nature of Cognition Knowledge is essentially social and collaborative
NK-3 Deep Uncertainty Cognition must face situations where outcomes, probabilities, and utilities are all uncertain

Unity of the New Three Views

The New Three Views are not three independent perspectives, but a coherent whole:

Together, they answer: In such a universe, what does life mean? What does cognition mean? How should we act?
