Research Agenda: Open Questions and Future Directions

Interstellar Civilization Studies is an emerging discipline with numerous open questions awaiting research. This section outlines short-term, medium-term, and long-term research agendas, as well as key open questions facing the discipline, inviting interested scholars to participate in this academic exploration oriented toward humanity's and the universe's future.

Short-term Research Agenda (5-10 Years)

Direction Specific Content Priority
Theoretical Deepening Refine philosophical arguments for the New Three Views and respond to criticisms High
Indicator Validation Conduct empirical validation and calibration of the six major indicators High
Institutional Piloting Pilot the protocol stack methodology in specific domains Medium
Discipline Building Establish academic journals, societies, and curriculum systems Medium
Policy Integration Transform framework into policy recommendations Medium

Medium-term Research Agenda (10-30 Years)

Direction Specific Content Conditional Dependencies
Institutional Implementation Promote actual establishment of core institutions Political will
International Integration Interface with international governance systems International cooperation
Technological Integration Synchronize with AI and space technology development Technological progress
Inter-civilizational Preparation Prepare for possible inter-civilizational contact SETI progress

Long-term Research Agenda (30+ Years)

Vision Specific Content
Disciplinary Maturity Become a recognized interdisciplinary field
Institutional Implementation Core institutions become part of global governance
Paradigmatic Influence New Three Views become more widely accepted worldview
Inter-civilizational Application Provide theoretical preparation for possible inter-civilizational contact

Key Open Questions

Conceptual Level

Question Content Priority
Nature of Consciousness What is the nature of consciousness? How to measure it? High
Possibility Space How to precisely define and measure possibility space? High
Negentropy Responsibility Allocation How to allocate negentropy responsibility among different agents? Medium
Cross-substrate Agency Status How to assess agency status of non-carbon-based entities? Medium

Methodological Level

Question Content Priority
Deep Time Prediction How to make meaningful predictions on deep time scales? High
Deep Uncertainty Decision-making How to make reasonable decisions under deep uncertainty? High
Indicator Computation How to make indicator computation feasible? Medium

Application Level

Question Content Priority
Policy Translation How to translate theory into feasible policies? High
Institutional Integration How to integrate with existing institutions? High
International Cooperation How to promote adoption at the global level? Medium

Invitation to Participate

Interstellar Civilization Studies welcomes researchers from diverse backgrounds to participate:

  • Philosophers: Deepen ontological and epistemological foundations
  • Ethicists: Develop and critique normative frameworks
  • Scientists: Validate and calibrate indicator systems
  • Policy Researchers: Develop policy applications
  • Technical Experts: Explore technical implementations
  • Social Scientists: Study social acceptance and implementation challenges
