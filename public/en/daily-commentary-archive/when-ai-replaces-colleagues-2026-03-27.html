馃幆 Recommendations & Outlook

For policymakers: Before the space for state-level experimentation is fully closed, establish a traceable "regulatory rollback pathway" 鈥?that is, a defined mechanism for reactivating multi-layered regulatory structures when the federal framework proves insufficient. For individuals and organizations: When AI's capability curve visibly outpaces the governance curve, the most rational response is not to wait for institutions to provide answers, but to actively raise the questions institutions are not yet equipped to address: Who has the authority to determine the pace at which AI displaces human work?
