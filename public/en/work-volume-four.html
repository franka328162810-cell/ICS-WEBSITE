Volume IV: Appendices and Resources
Technical Support and Extension Gateway for the Theory

Volume IV of Normative Foundations of Interstellar Civilization serves as the technical support and resource repository for the entire four-volume work, providing detailed terminology definitions, argumentation details, calculation methods, comparative analyses, and teaching resources for the first three volumes. This volume is not a simple compilation of appendices, but a carefully designed knowledge management system that enables readers to deeply understand theoretical details, verify argumentation validity, apply operational tools, and conduct teaching and research.

Core Mission

This volume is divided into three major parts, systematically providing technical support and extension resources for the first three volumes:

Technical Appendices (Appendices A-F) provide the technical foundation of the theory; Comparative Appendices (Appendices G-J) place this work in the context of international academic discourse; Resource Appendices (Appendices K-M) support teaching and research.

Technical Appendices (A-F)

Provide the technical foundation of the theory, including detailed terminology definitions, argumentation details, calculation methods, and technical specifications:

Appendix A
Core Glossary
Contains 300+ core terms, each with formalized definitions, natural language explanations, first appearance locations, related terms, application examples, and common misconceptions
Appendix B
Argumentation Node Summary
Extracts 1000+ argumentation nodes from the entire work, each containing core conclusions, argumentation basis, supporting evidence, potential rebuttals, and response strategies
Appendix C
Indicator Calculation Guide
Provides detailed calculation methods for six major indicator systems, including formula derivations, parameter explanations, data requirements, calculation examples, and error analysis
Appendix D
Protocol Stack Rules Compilation
Provides complete technical specifications for institutional design, including detailed rules for L₀-L₄ layers, interface definitions, exception handling, and version management
Appendix E
Simulation Model Technical Documentation
Provides complete code, parameter settings, operation instructions, and result interpretations for simulation models used in stress testing, allowing researchers to reproduce simulation experiments
Appendix F
Formalized Proof Compilation
Provides formalized proofs of key propositions using first-order logic and modal logic, ensuring logical rigor of argumentation

These technical appendices enable readers to trace the argumentation chain of any conclusion, verify argumentation validity, apply indicator systems, implement institutional designs, reproduce simulation experiments, and validate logical rigor.

Comparative Appendices (G-J)

Place this work in the context of international academic discourse, clarifying the theory's unique contributions and academic positioning:

Appendix G
Comparison with International Space Law
Compares the institutional designs of this work with existing international space law (Outer Space Treaty, Moon Agreement, Planetary Protection Policy) clause by clause, identifying complementarities and conflicts
Appendix H
Comparison with AI Ethics Literature
Compares the AI governance framework of this work with mainstream AI ethics principles (IEEE, EU, OECD), clarifying the unique contributions of this work
Appendix I
Comparison with Longtermism Literature
Compares in detail the core views of this work with longtermist scholars such as MacAskill, Ord, and Bostrom, clarifying conceptual differences and normative divergences
Appendix J
Minimal Argumentation Path
Provides a simplified version of the entire work's argumentation, presenting in approximately 10,000 words the core argumentation chain from problems to New Three Views, from New Three Views to normative principles, and from normative principles to institutional design

Appendix J "Minimal Argumentation Path" is a shortcut to quickly understanding the logic of the entire work and a focal point for critical examination. Through these comparative analyses, readers can clearly see the position and unique value of this work at the international academic frontier.

Resource Appendices (K-M)

Support teaching and research, providing educators and researchers with rich practical resources:

Appendix K
Teaching Resource Package
Includes three complete course syllabi (undergraduate, graduate, short-term training), 20 teaching cases, standardized PPT and handout templates, 8 classroom exercises and assignment designs, and recommended video resource links
Appendix L
Research Agenda and Open Questions
Systematically organizes 35 open questions, categorized by short-term (1-5 years), medium-term (5-10 years), and long-term (10-20 years), each including problem statement, research significance, possible methods, expected difficulties, and related literature
Appendix M
Extended Reading Guide
Recommends 300+ references by topic (AI ethics, intergenerational justice, extraterrestrial contact, environmental ethics, etc.), each with brief annotations and explanations of connections to this work

Teaching Case Examples

Each teaching case is carefully designed, including scenario descriptions, discussion questions, framework application guides, and reference answers. This enables educators to quickly develop Interstellar Civilization Studies courses, transforming abstract theory into vivid teaching practice.

The research agenda provides researchers with rich research topics, from conceptual clarification to empirical testing, from theoretical refinement to policy application, covering all aspects of Interstellar Civilization Studies.

Knowledge Management System

The design of this volume embodies the concept of "knowledge engineering": treating academic knowledge as manageable, retrievable, and reusable resources.

Complete Index System

  • Subject Index — Organizes all content by thematic classification
  • Name Index — Quickly locates all cited scholars and authors
  • Term Index — Locates all term definitions and usage
  • Figure Index — Compiles all figures and visualizations
  • Formula Index — Organizes all mathematical formulas and calculation methods
