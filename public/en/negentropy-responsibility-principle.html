Negentropy Responsibility Principle: Justice as High-Dimensional Negentropy

The Negentropy Responsibility Principle asserts that intelligent agents bear responsibility for protecting and enhancing the universe's negentropy reserves, avoiding irreversible large-scale depletion. This principle extends ethical responsibility to the cosmic physical level, providing a new perspective for understanding justice and value.

Principle Statement

Negentropy Responsibility Principle (NRP)

Intelligent agents bear responsibility for protecting and enhancing the universe's negentropy reserves, and should avoid irreversibly depleting negentropy on a large scale.

Formal Expression
d(Negentropyuniverse)
Obligation: ──────────────── ≥ −ε

Where:
  • Negentropyuniverse: Total negentropy in the universe (or accessible portion)
  • ε: Permissible rate of negentropy depletion
  • Principle requirement: The rate of negentropy depletion should not exceed a certain threshold

Central Role of Negentropy

What is Negentropy?

Negentropy = Negative entropy = Order = Information

  • Second Law of Thermodynamics: Entropy always increases in isolated systems
  • Life and Civilization: Exist through maintaining and growing local negentropy
  • Negentropy is scarce and valuable in the universe

Relationship Between Negentropy and Value

Negentropy is related to almost all values we care about:

Value Relationship with Negentropy
Life Life is the local maintenance of negentropy
Complexity Complexity is a form of negentropy
Diversity Diversity is a distribution characteristic of negentropy
Freedom Freedom requires negentropy as material foundation
Possibility Possibility space depends on negentropy

Core Maxim

"Justice is High-Dimensional Negentropy"

This maxim reveals that justice is not an abstract moral concept, but order in high dimensions (social, political, ethical dimensions)—a form of negentropy.

Justification of the Principle

Derivation from New Life View

The Negentropy Responsibility Principle can be derived from the New Life View:

Argument Steps:

  • The essence of life is the maintenance and growth of local negentropy (NL-2)
  • Negentropy is a necessary condition for life's continuation
  • We value life (including future life)
  • Conclusion: We have a responsibility to protect negentropy—the material foundation of life

Derivation from Recursive Freedom Principle

The Negentropy Responsibility Principle can also be derived from the Recursive Freedom Principle:

Argument Steps:

  • Should not systematically weaken the conditions of freedom for future agents (RFP)
  • Negentropy is the material foundation for exercising freedom
  • Large-scale irreversible depletion of negentropy will weaken the conditions of freedom for future agents
  • Conclusion: Should avoid irreversibly depleting negentropy on a large scale

Practical Guidelines

How Does the Negentropy Responsibility Principle Guide Action?

Domain Guidance
Resource Use Prioritize renewable resources, avoid irreversible consumption
Technology Development Develop technologies with high negentropy efficiency
Environmental Protection Protect ecosystems—important storage of negentropy
Civilization Development Pursue sustainable development paths

Relationship with Sustainable Development

The Negentropy Responsibility Principle provides a deeper theoretical foundation for sustainable development:

  • The essence of sustainable development is the sustainable use of negentropy
  • Not just resource sustainability, but sustainability of possibility space
  • Extends sustainable development to cosmic scale

Levels of Negentropy Responsibility

Negentropy responsibility can be understood at different levels:

Level Content Responsibility
Individual Level Personal energy and resource consumption Responsible consumption
Social Level Social production and distribution methods Sustainable social organization
Civilization Level Civilization's development path Sustainable civilization model
Cosmic Level Use of cosmic negentropy Cosmic responsibility ethics

Relationship with Forbidden Red Lines

The Negentropy Responsibility Principle is directly related to Forbidden Red Line FRL-2:

FRL-2: Prohibition of irreversible large-scale depletion of cosmic negentropy reserves

The Negentropy Responsibility Principle is a progressive requirement, while the Forbidden Red Line is an absolute prohibition:

Negentropy Responsibility Principle (NRP) Forbidden Red Line (FRL-2)
Should reduce negentropy depletion Absolutely prohibits large-scale irreversible depletion
Can be balanced and adjusted Cannot be balanced, absolutely prohibited
Guides daily decisions Defines boundaries that cannot be crossed
