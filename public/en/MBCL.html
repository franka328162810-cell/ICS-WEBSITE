Memetic Biosafety Containment Level: Risk Management of Information Entities

The Memetic Biosafety Containment Level (MBCL) is an innovative indicator that extends biosafety classification methods to information entities—including AI systems, algorithms, memes (cultural genes), and ideologies. In an era of increasingly powerful information technology, certain information entities may pose systemic risks similar to biological pathogens, requiring appropriate safety controls.

Why Do We Need MBCL?

Risks of Information Entities

Certain information entities—such as specific AI systems, algorithms, and ideologies—may possess characteristics similar to "pathogens":

Characteristic Biological Pathogen Information "Pathogen"
Transmissibility Spreads through contact Spreads through information channels
Replicability Replicates in host Replicates in minds/systems
Destructiveness Damages host health Damages cognitive autonomy or social function
Mutability Undergoes mutation Undergoes mutation and adaptation
Resistance May develop drug resistance May develop resistance to countermeasures

Biosafety Level Analogy

Biosafety laboratories have four levels (BSL-1 to BSL-4), corresponding to different risk levels. MBCL extends this framework to the information domain:

If we recognize that certain information entities can spread and cause harm like pathogens, then we equally need safety levels and control measures.

Definition of MBCL

Definition:

The Memetic Biosafety Containment Level (MBCL) is a classification framework for assessing the risk level of information entities (AI systems, algorithms, memes, ideologies, etc.) and determining corresponding safety control measures.

Level System

Level Risk Level Characteristics Control Requirements
MBCL-1 Low Risk Limited impact, reversible, controllable Basic monitoring and recording
MBCL-2 Medium Risk Some transmissibility and influence, but controllable Controlled dissemination, regular review
MBCL-3 High Risk High transmissibility, may cause serious impact Strict isolation, professional monitoring
MBCL-4 Extreme Risk May cause irreversible systemic impact Maximum protection, special review

Risk Assessment Dimensions

MBCL assessment is based on the following dimensions:

Dimension Assessment Content Description
Transmission Speed Speed and scope of information entity spread Global instant spread vs. local slow spread
Impact Depth Degree of impact on individual cognition and behavior Surface impact vs. deep cognitive change
Reversibility Degree of reversibility of impact Easily correctable vs. irreversible change
Autonomy Violation Degree of violation of cognitive autonomy Preserves autonomous judgment vs. deprives autonomy
Systemic Risk Possibility of triggering systemic social risks Isolated impact vs. cascading effects
Mutability Ability of information entity to mutate and evolve Stable vs. rapid mutation

Level Details and Examples

MBCL-1 (Low Risk)

Feature Content
Description Information entities with limited, reversible, and controllable impact
Examples Ordinary advertising, simple recommendation algorithms, general opinions
Control Basic monitoring and information literacy education

MBCL-2 (Medium Risk)

Feature Content
Description Some transmissibility and influence, but controllable through standard measures
Examples Highly optimized recommendation algorithms, influential ideologies, precision advertising
Control Controlled dissemination, transparency requirements, regular review

MBCL-3 (High Risk)

Feature Content
Description High transmissibility, may cause serious cognitive or social impact
Examples Deepfake technology, highly manipulative algorithms, extreme ideology dissemination systems
Control Strict isolation, professional monitoring, ethical review, usage restrictions

MBCL-4 (Extreme Risk)

Feature Content
Description May cause irreversible systemic social or cognitive impact
Examples Advanced AI manipulation systems, systemic cognitive control technology, uncontrollable autonomous AI
Control Maximum protection, multiple safety layers, special ethics review committee, possible prohibition

Relationship with Forbidden Red Lines

MBCL-4 level information entities may touch the Forbidden Red Line FRL-1 (systemic violation of consciousness sovereignty):

MBCL Level Relationship with FRL
MBCL-1,2 Usually below the forbidden red line
MBCL-3 Need to be vigilant about approaching the forbidden red line
MBCL-4 May touch FRL-1, requires strictest review
