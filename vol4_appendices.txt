--- A.3 NORMATIVE TERMS ---
A.3.1 Recursive Freedom (递归自由)
  - RFP: ∀a∈Actions, ∀S∈Subjects: Permissible(a) ⟺ ¬∃S': ΔP(S'|a) < -ε (systematic)
  - "自由是对可能性的看护" / "Freedom is the Stewardship of Possibility"
  - Features: recursive, possibility-oriented, deep-time, multi-agent

A.3.2 Possibility Space (规范性维度)
  - P^norm(S,t) = {s'∈P(S,t): Valuable(s') ∨ EnablingValuable(s')}
  - RFD(a,S) = (|P(S,t₁)| - |P(S,t₀)|) / |P(S,t₀)|

A.3.3 Negentropy Responsibility (负熵责任)
  - NRP: NegentropyCapability(S) ⇒ NegentropyObligation(S)
  - Physical basis (thermodynamics), cosmic scale, existential

A.3.4 Reversibility (可逆性)
  - Reversibility(a) ∈ [0,1], exists a⁻¹ such that d(S(t₀), S(t₀')) < ε
  - REV ⟸ Reversibility

A.3.5 Forbidden Red Lines (禁忌红线/FRL)

4. APPENDIX J - MINIMAL ARGUMENT PATH (最小论证路径)
================================================================

MAP = min|Path| such that Path ⊢ Conclusions

--- CORE PROBLEM (3 puzzles) ---
Puzzle 1: What grounds normativity in a cosmos without pre-given purposes?
Puzzle 2: How to be rational under deep uncertainty?
Puzzle 3: How to attribute responsibility across deep time?

--- PATH: PROBLEM → THREE VIEWS ---
Step 1: Core problem exists (normative framework needed for interstellar scenarios)
Step 2: Existing approaches insufficient (traditional ethics, longtermism, global governance, complexity science)
Step 3: New framework needed → Three Views (NC, NL, NK)

Three Views Core Propositions:
NC: NC-1 Relation≻Entity, NC-2 Process≻State, NC-3 Participation≻Observation
NL: NL-1 Life is cross-substrate, NL-2 Life≡Negentropy Organization, NL-3 Agenthood is continuous
NK: NK-1 Cognition≡Participation, NK-2 Knowledge≡Social Construction, NK-3 Deep Uncertainty exists, NK-4 Norms≡Negotiated

--- PATH: THREE VIEWS → PRINCIPLES ---
Bridge Principles:
  BP-1: Normativity presupposes survival
  BP-2: Survival depends on conditions
  BP-3: Maintaining conditions is rational
  Conclusion: Normativity → Maintain(SurvivalConditions)

Derivation:
  BP + NC + NL → RFP (protect possibility space)
  BP + NC + NL-2 → NRP (maintain negentropy resources)
  BP + NK-3 → REV (prefer reversible actions under deep uncertainty)

Principle Hierarchy:
  RFP (highest/目标原则) > NRP (support/资源原则) > REV (operational/策略原则)
  In conflict: follow this priority order

--- PATH: PRINCIPLES → INSTITUTIONS ---
RFP → Multi-Intelligence Tribunal (主体保护)
NRP → Cosmic-Scale IRB + Intergenerational Trust (负熵维护)
REV → Versioned Governance + Rollback (可逆治理)
Integration → Protocol Stack 5-layer architecture

--- PATH: INSTITUTIONS → INDICATORS ---
RFP → UCS (主体确认) + RFD (自由度量)
NRP → CDI (文明维护)
REV → CRV (承诺可逆) + MBCL (信息风险) + CSIA (影响评估)

Three-layer indicator architecture:
  Foundation: UCS, CDI (基础层)
  Assessment: RFD, CRV, MBCL (评估层)
  Integration: CSIA (整合层)
